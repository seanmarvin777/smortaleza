OUR STORY
A Legacy of Leadership and Impact reflects our continuing commitment to developing empowered young leaders who create meaningful and lasting change in the community. Through service projects, leadership programs, and collaborative initiatives, we build not only capable individuals but also stronger partnerships and sustainable solutions. Each generation of members carries forward the mission — to lead with purpose, serve with integrity, and leave a positive mark for those who follow.
1973
JCI Silang Pasimuno was officially chartered, with 28 founding members led by President Art K. Belamide. It marked the beginning of a legacy rooted in service and leadership.
1998
In a proud and historic moment, JCI Silang Pasimuno took the helm of the entire Silang Town Fiesta, serving as the Executive Committee. It was a showcase of their organizational strength, unity, and deep connection to the local community.
2023
JCI Silang Pasimuno celebrated its 50th founding anniversary, honoring five decades of impactful projects, empowered leaders, and unwavering commitment to the community.
2025
The legacy lives on in its 52nd year, as JCI Silang Pasimuno continues to ignite change and empower future leaders, staying true to its mission of service to humanity.
