Our Story

About JCI Silang Pasimuno

A community of young leaders committed to positive change, innovation, and sustainable development in Silang and beyond.

Who We Are

Building Tomorrow's Leaders Today

JCI Silang Pasimuno is a vibrant local chapter of the Junior Chamber International, bringing together passionate young leaders aged 18-40 who are driven to create meaningful impact in our community.

Founded on the principles of leadership development, community service, and international cooperation, we provide our members with opportunities to grow personally and professionally while addressing the most pressing needs of Silang and surrounding areas.

Our chapter is recognized for excellence in project implementation, member engagement, and measurable community impact. We believe that young people have the power to drive positive change, and we channel that energy into programs that make a real difference.

👁️

Our Vision

To be the leading organization of young active citizens in Silang, recognized for transforming lives and communities through sustainable leadership and innovative service programs.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower young leaders to create positive change in themselves and their communities through active citizenship, leadership development, and collaborative service projects that address real social needs.

💎

Our Values

Integrity, innovation, excellence, compassion, and accountability guide everything we do. We are committed to transparent governance, measurable impact, and sustainable solutions.

Our Journey

Milestones That Define Us

1973

Chapter Establishment

JCI Silang Pasimuno was officially chartered, with 28 founding members led by President Art K. Belamide. It marked the beginning of a legacy rooted in service and leadership.

1998

First Local Recognition

In a proud and historic moment, JCI Silang Pasimuno took the helm of the entire Silang Town Fiesta, serving as the Executive Committee. It was a showcase of their organizational strength, unity, and deep connection to the local community.

2023

50th Anniversary

JCI Silang Pasimuno celebrated its 50th founding anniversary, honoring five decades of impactful projects, empowered leaders, and unwavering commitment to the community.

2025

Platinum Recognition

Achieved Platinum Seal of Efficiency, Platinum Banner Award, and Top 50 Local Organization status, cementing our position as one the most impactful and effective JCI chapters in the Philippines for the year 2025

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick Answers

What is JCI Silang Pasimuno?

We are a local chapter of Junior Chamber International (JCI), a global organization of young active citizens. We focus on leadership development and community service in Silang, Cavite.

How often do you meet?

We hold regular chapter meetings twice a month, plus project-specific gatherings and special events throughout the year.

Do I need experience to join?

No prior experience is necessary! We welcome members from all backgrounds. Training and mentorship are provided to help you grow.

How can I support if I can't join as a member?

You can volunteer for specific projects, make donations, partner with us, or help spread the word about our initiatives. Every contribution matters!

What projects are you currently running?

We have 10+ active projects covering education, environment, mental health, legal awareness, disaster response, and more. Check our Projects page for details.

How is JCI Silang Pasimuno funded?

We're funded through membership dues, fundraising events, corporate sponsorships, and individual donations. All funds directly support our community projects.
